About AquaPond Guide

An independent, plain-English resource for koi ponds, water quality, fish health and aquatic plants.

AquaPond Guide was created for a simple reason: most pond advice online is either too technical for beginners or simply wrong. We write the guides we wish we had when we started — practical, step-by-step, and honest about what actually matters.

What we cover

  • Pond basics & water quality — cycling, parameters, stocking and seasonal care.
  • Fish health — recognising problems early and safe first steps.
  • Equipment — pumps, filters and UV units, sized correctly.
  • Plants — pond and aquarium plants that actually survive.

Our approach

  • Beginner first. Every guide assumes you are starting from zero.
  • Prevention over treatment. Clear water and healthy fish are 90% about getting the basics right.
  • Honest limits. We tell you when a pond is too small, a filter too weak, or a stocking level too high — even when it is not what you wanted to hear.

Editorial note

Guides are written and reviewed by experienced pond and aquarium keepers, based on established aquatic-keeping practice. Fish-health articles are for general information only and never replace professional veterinary advice — when in doubt, consult a qualified aquatic veterinarian or pond specialist.
