Best Time of Day to Feed Koi

Feeding~6 min readUpdated Nov 17, 2026

Feeding is the most hands-on part of pond keeping, and the timing is easier than people make it. Koi are most active and digest best when the water is comfortably warm and the day is well underway. A simple, consistent routine beats guessing. (See what to feed in the koi feeding guide.)

When koi eat best

Koi are cold-blooded, so their digestion follows the water temperature rather than the clock. Once the sun has warmed the pond a little, usually mid-morning, they tend to be alert and ready to feed. Feeding too early, before the water has had a chance to warm, often means food sits uneaten and pollutes the pond.

How often

Water conditionsTypical feeding routine
Warm, growing seasonOnce or twice a day, only what they finish quickly
Cooling autumnCut back, switch to cool-water food (see cold-water feeding)
Cold winterStop feeding once the water stays cold
Rule of thumbFeed only what the koi finish within a few minutes. Any leftover food sinks, rots and loads the filter — overfeeding is far more common than underfeeding.

What to watch

If the koi ignore the food, do not keep adding more. Loss of appetite can mean cold water, stress, or an underlying problem (see koi not eating). Watch their behavior at each feeding — it is the quickest daily health check you get.
